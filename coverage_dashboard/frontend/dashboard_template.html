How this dashboard is built.

The logic behind every number — what goes into pipe and bookings, and the math that turns them into coverage and needed coverage. Full detail (including the raw queries) lives in planning/PLAN.md.

A At a glance

Two sources, two dollar metrics — never mixed. Pipe is always Cal_IACV (gross); booked is always Product_NACV = NACV_USD − Uplift_USD (net). They reconcile to different reports: pipe → PBI open-pipe; booked → Historic.xlsx / PBI bookings.

B Pipe — the logic

Open pipe = sum of Cal_IACV for opps in an Open stage, observed weekly. LS pipe is the same, restricted to late stages (Exec Presentation, Technical Evaluation, Negotiation).

Stage buckets

BucketFrom
Open → open pipeactive funnel (Discovery → Negotiation)
Closed Won → booked sideClosed Won, Stage 5, & 6-Closed/Pending
Closed / Other → ignoredLost, Deferred, admin / dup / churn / rejected

In / out & geo

  • Excluded: non-quota teams (Account Management, Global, QAS AM, null), zero-value rows, and admin / dup / churn / rejected / renewal-outreach / first-interaction stages.
  • Geo from the booking team's region family → AMS / APAC / EMEA / Public Sector.

Week pinning

  • Every week → beginning-of-week balance — the earliest in-quarter snapshot in the week (the standing pipe at the start of the week). Week 1 is the start-of-quarter snapshot (Apr-1 for Q2, Jan-1 for Q1) = PBI's "starting pipe" ($106.1M ≈ PBI $106.18M; Q1 $104.8M).
  • Exception — the in-flight current week → latest snapshot (today's standing pipe), tying to gtm-weekly-reporting's weekly number to the dollar (FY26 Q2 wk9 = $46.78M). Past weeks stay on their week-start balance.

C Bookings — the logic

Booked uses snapshot Closed-Won Cal_IACV for weeks 1–12 (the same gross metric as open pipe, matching gtm-weekly-reporting), and the live Product_NACV total at week 13 (NACV_USD − Uplift_USD, the true final bookings that tie to Historic.xlsx/PBI). The in-flight quarter (no week 13 yet) shows snapshot booked throughout.

  • Included: 6-Closed/Pending counts as booked (both paths; ≈ $0.43M of FY26 Q2). On the live path, NPA negative adjustments are kept (they net de-bookings / credits).
  • Excluded: non-quota teams, zero rows, any non-won/pending stage, and deal types outside New Business / Expansion / Upsell / Professional Services.
  • Trade-off: snapshot Closed-Won Cal_IACV understates historic bookings vs live (FY26 Q1 $15.72M snapshot vs $16.63M Historic.xlsx) — kept for metric consistency with open pipe + a frozen series.

D The math

Coverage

LTB = target − booked coverage = open_pipe ÷ LTB (— when LTB ≤ 0)

3× = carry $3 of open pipe for every $1 still left to book.

Needed coverage (from history)

For each closed quarter, geo, deal type and week N — what fraction of the open pipe sitting there at week N had booked by quarter-close:

conversion(N) = ( final_booked − booked_at_N ) ÷ open_pipe_at_N rec_coverage(N) = 1 ÷ median( conversion across closed quarters )

Median (not mean) across the ~9 closed quarters (FY24 Q1 – FY26 Q1). e.g. 33% → 3.0×, 22% → 4.5×. The needed multiple is a curve — high early (pipe still holds eventual-losers), lower near quarter-end — which a single 1 ÷ win-rate can't capture.

Needed pipe (drawn on the chart)

needed_pipe(N) = LTB(N) × rec_coverage(N)

E What we changed

  • Week pinning: each week shows its beginning-of-week standing pipe (week 1 = start-of-quarter = PBI "starting pipe", Q2 $106.1M). The in-flight current week uses the latest snapshot (GTM tie, $46.78M).
  • Booked: snapshot for weeks 1–12, live total at week 13. The weekly progression uses snapshot Closed-Won Cal_IACV (consistent with open pipe + matches gtm-weekly-reporting); only the final week (13) shows the live Product_NACV total (true final bookings, ties Historic.xlsx).
  • 6-Closed/Pending counted as booked — confirmed & documented (≈ $0.43M of FY26 Q2).
  • One-decimal display on the weekly detail and recommended-coverage tables.
  • Removed the old Overview page; added this Methodology page.

Recommendation

Needed coverage = 1 / median historic conversion at week N. Conversion = open pipe at week N that closes by EOQ, across closed FY24/FY25/FY26-Q1 quarters.

Method

Each week's pipe = the beginning-of-week snapshot (latest snapshot for the in-flight current week). Booked = snapshot Stage='Closed Won' for weeks 1–12, live SKU NACV total at week 13. Needed pipe at week N = (target − booked) / conversion(N).
